Minimal stroked line icons (Lucide-derived) for small accents and faint background motifs.

```jsx
<LineIcon name="scale" size={64} color="var(--accent)" />
```

- Names: `scale`, `ship`, `truck`, `car`, `document`, `container`, `globe`, `alert`.
- Keep usage sparing — icons are texture, not decoration. Stroke 2 by default.
