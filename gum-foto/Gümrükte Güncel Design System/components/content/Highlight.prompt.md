The yellow "tweezers" accent — wrap a single keyword or number for emphasis.

```jsx
<p>Çoğu kişi bunu <Highlight>masum</Highlight> sanıyor.</p>
<h1>En <Highlight variant="fill">pahalı</Highlight> hata</h1>
```

- `variant="text"` (default) = yellow text; `variant="fill"` = yellow block, navy ink (strongest).
- Use at most once per post (numbers via `StatNumber` excepted). Yellow is precious.
